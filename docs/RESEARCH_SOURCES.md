# CampusBite Design & Research Sources

This document tracks reference systems, institutional canteen archetypes, and design pattern sources analyzed during Phase 06.

---

## 1. Food Systems & Institutional Canteens Studied
1. **Anna University & IIT Madras Campus Canteens (Chennai)**: High-volume breakfast steam lines, token-based lunch counters, filter coffee stations.
2. **Saravana Bhavan & Murugan Idli Shop Menus**: Classical South Indian tiffin naming conventions, sambar vada platters, ghee podi dosa varieties.
3. **Hot Chips & Tea Post (Student Hubs)**: Evening snack models, samosa chaat, bread omelette, fresh juices, milkshakes.
4. **Juice World & Lassi Shop (University Clusters)**: Fresh fruit juice varieties (ABC, Mosambi, Watermelon, Nannari Sarbath), thick shakes (Oreo, Belgian Chocolate).
5. **Tibbs Frankie & Roll Baby Roll**: Fast-service kathi roll and wrap packaging and pricing models.

---

## 2. Digital UI & Ordering Systems Studied
1. **Toast POS Web Ordering & Kitchen Display System**: Order tokenization, ticket aging, real-time counter fulfillment workflows.
2. **Square for Restaurants & Kiosk Architecture**: Multi-category touch navigation, large touch target buttons, instant modifier handling.
3. **Blue Bottle & Sweetgreen Web Experiences**: High-craft culinary photography ratios, tabular numeric typography, restrained header navigation.
4. **Figma Community Food & Restaurant UI Kits**: Mobile bottom sheets, stepper interactions, dietary indicator placements.

---

## 3. Copyright & Originality Compliance
- **Zero Asset Cloning**: No third-party proprietary photography, SVG brand marks, or copy was copied verbatim.
- **Custom Design Implementation**: All CSS tokens, React components, and interaction logic were custom-engineered from first principles for CampusBite.
